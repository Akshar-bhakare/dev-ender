# SyncUp: Design Vision & Visual Language

SyncUp is designed to be the premier "trusted professional ecosystem," balancing the social connectivity of LinkedIn with the rigorous verification needs of modern businesses and investors.

## 1. Core Visual Strategy

### 🎨 Color Palette (Light Theme)
- **Background**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) and `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) to distinguish content layers.
- **Primary Action**: `#2563EB` (Royal Blue) – conveys trust, authority, and professionalism.
- **Text**: `#0F172A` (Slate 900) for high contrast and readability.
- **Verification Accents**: 
    - **Face Verified**: Emerald Green (`#10B981`) – represents "Go" and safety.
    - **Company Verified**: Gold (`#F59E0B`) – represents prestige and established status.
- **Surface**: Subtle borders (`#E2E8F0`) instead of heavy shadows for a refined, modern look.

### ✍️ Typography
- **Primary Font**: `Inter` or `Geist Sans` (Modern, neutral, excellent legibility for data-heavy sections).
- **Secondary Font**: `Outfit` for headers to add a touch of "tech-forward" personality.

---

## 2. The Professional Feed Experience

The feed is the heart of SyncUp. Unlike traditional social feeds, every item in a SyncUp feed is a "verified professional interaction."

### 📰 Post Components
- **Identity Header**: Shows the user's name, role, and most importantly, their **Verification Shield** (Face/Identity icons).
- **Visual Content**: High DPI image support with elegant rounded corners (`12px`).
- **Subtle Interactions**: Clean, monochromatic icons for Like/Comment/Share that only gain color state on interaction to keep the UI quiet and focused.

### 💰 Inline Promotions (Ad Engine)
- **Design Strategy**: Sponsored content will be styled identically to organic posts to prevent "banner blindness," but marked with a clear, professional "Sponsored" tag and a call-to-action button (e.g., "Learn More" or "Book Now").
- **Constraint**: No flashy animations or distracting colors – professional ads only.

### 💼 Career Pulse (Job Recommendations)
- **Design Strategy**: Compact, horizontal cards inserted between every few posts.
- **Focus**: Targeted at *experienced* professionals – showing salary ranges and mission statements rather than just job titles.

---

## 3. Trusted Verification Layer

The UI explicitly highlights "Trust Signals" across the platform to solve the impersonation and fraud issues identified in `ps.md`.

- **Personal Verification**: A distinct checkmark next to names that, when hovered, explains the verification level (Liveness Check, Identity Document).
- **Company Legitimacy**: Company pages feature a "Business Registry Verified" badge, linking to official records where applicable.
- **Event Escrow**: Paid events show an "Escrow Protected" badge, reassuring users that funds are held until event completion.

---

## 4. B2B & Opportunity Discovery

A dedicated layout for non-social interactions:
- **Project Boards**: A clean, list-based view for company-to-company project bidding.
- **Investment Hub**: Founders see an AI-curated list of verified investors, and vice versa, with direct, gated communication channels.

---

## 5. UI Mockup Previews (Implementation Idea)

- **Navigation**: Top-bar centric for accessibility, with a secondary "Quick Actions" sidebar for AI suggestions and Referral tracking.
- **Dashboard**: A "Mission Control" feel when managing ads or events – using data visualizations (clean line charts/bar graphs) to show reach and engagement.
